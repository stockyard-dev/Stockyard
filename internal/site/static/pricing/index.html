Frequently asked questions

What's in a bundle?

Each bundle is 5-10 tools picked for a specific community — therapists, barber shops, minecraft servers, dog breeders, and 190+ more. Visit stockyard.dev and type what you do to see yours.

What's the difference between Local and Cloud?

Local is the complete product on your machine — every tool, every bundle, no account required. Cloud adds encrypted backup and cross-device sync. The tools are identical. Full comparison here.

Is there a free trial?

No free trial on the desktop tiers — the prices are straight purchase. The free, open-source LLM proxy is available if you want to kick the tires on that first. Local is $299 one-time; Cloud Single starts at $49/mo, Cloud Multi at $129/mo. If you buy and need help, email hello@stockyard.dev.

Is the proxy really free?

Yes. The open-source proxy is Apache 2.0. Free forever. No limits. No phone-home. 38 LLM providers, 76 middleware modules. Download it here.

Where does my data live?

On your machine. In SQLite files. Back up by copying the folder. We never see your data. Cloud tiers add optional encrypted backup to our servers, but the local copy is always the source of truth.
