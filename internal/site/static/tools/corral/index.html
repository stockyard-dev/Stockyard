Stockyard Corral

Webhook Debugger & Relay

Receive, inspect, replay, and forward webhooks. Self-hosted RequestBin in a single binary.

The problem

You are integrating with Stripe or GitHub. The webhook fired but your endpoint was down. Or your handler crashed and you need to replay the payload.

How Corral works

Corral creates endpoints that receive webhooks, log the full request, and forward them to your application. Every webhook is stored and replayable.

# Create an endpoint
curl -X POST localhost:8800/api/endpoints \
  -d '{"name":"stripe","forward_to":"http://localhost:3000/hook"}'

# Replay a delivery
curl -X POST localhost:8800/api/deliveries/del_abc/replay

Features

Unlimited endpoints. Full request logging. Real-time SSE dashboard. Replay any delivery. Forward to multiple destinations. Retry with backoff. Filter and search.
