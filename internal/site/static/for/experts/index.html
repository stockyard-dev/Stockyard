For Domain Experts

Turn your expertise into passive income.

Create a knowledge base. Apps subscribe. You earn royalties on every query.

The Problem

You have decades of specialized knowledge — medical protocols, legal precedents, financial regulations, industry best practices. This knowledge would make AI applications dramatically better. But there's no way to contribute it or earn from it.

The Solution

Create a knowledge base on Stockyard. Structure your expertise as verified facts and entries. Apps on the platform subscribe to your knowledge base. When an app uses your data to answer a question, you earn a royalty. Your knowledge improves thousands of AI applications and you earn passive income from every query.

Key Features

Knowledge Marketplace

Publish your knowledge base. Set your price per query. Get discovered by app builders.

Royalty Earnings

80% of query fees go to you. Automatic payouts.

Community Verification

Other experts verify your entries. Verified entries rank higher and earn more.

Auto-Updates

Point Diffbot at your source URLs. Knowledge stays current automatically.

Reputation Building

Your contributor score grows with usage and verification. High reputation = featured placement.

Impact Tracking

See which apps use your knowledge, how many queries per day, and satisfaction scores.
