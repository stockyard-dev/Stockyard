The Problem

Every AI feature requires infrastructure: a proxy for routing, a logger for tracing, a cost tracker, safety filters, audit trails. Setting up six tools with Redis and Postgres takes weeks. You need to ship today.

The Solution

Stockyard is a single binary. Install in 30 seconds. Point your app at it. You get 58 middleware modules, cost tracking, caching, safety filters, and a full audit trail. No dependencies. No DevOps. Ship your AI feature today and optimize tomorrow.
