Protocol Specification

AI Commerce Protocol

An open specification for buying, selling, and settling AI inference in a decentralized marketplace.

Overview

Protocol Overview

The Stockyard AI Commerce Protocol (SACP) defines how participants in the AI marketplace discover services, negotiate pricing, execute transactions, and settle payments. It enables a trustless marketplace where providers and consumers transact without intermediaries.

  • Price discovery: Real-time bid/ask for inference capacity
  • Escrow: Funds held until inference is verified
  • Settlement: Automatic payment on successful completion
  • Disputes: Cryptographic proof for resolution
  • Reputation: On-chain quality and uptime scores

Transaction Flow

Transaction Lifecycle

01
Discovery
Consumer queries the marketplace for available providers, filtering by model, price, latency, and reputation score.
02
Quote
Provider returns a signed quote with price-per-token, estimated latency, and validity window.
03
Escrow
Consumer locks funds equal to the maximum cost estimate. Funds are held by the protocol.
04
Execution
Inference request is sent to the provider via the AI Inference Protocol (SAIP).
05
Verification
Response is hashed and signed. Both parties agree on token counts and quality metrics.
06
Settlement
Escrow releases actual cost to the provider. Remainder is returned to the consumer. Reputation scores update.
