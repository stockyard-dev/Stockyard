Self-Hosted Finance & Business Tools

Every tool is a single binary with embedded SQLite. No Docker, no Postgres, no external dependencies.

Financial data is the most sensitive category of business information. Invoice amounts reveal your pricing strategy. CRM records map your entire customer network. Expense reports expose your cost structure. Subscription data shows your revenue trends. Every SaaS finance tool — from FreshBooks to HubSpot to Expensify — has access to this data and uses it to benchmark your business against their other customers. Self-hosted finance tools keep this information private by design. The data lives in a SQLite file on your server, accessible only to the people and systems you authorize.

Where to start

Start with Billfold (invoicing) and Dossier (CRM) — these two tools handle the customer-facing financial workflows that contain the most sensitive data. Add Steward (expenses) and Books (bookkeeping) as your financial operations grow.
