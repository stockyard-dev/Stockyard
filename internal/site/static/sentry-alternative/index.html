Self-Hosted Sentry Alternatives in 2026

Sentry's Team plan starts at $26/mo. Here are self-hosted error tracking tools that run on your server without per-event pricing.

Why teams look for Sentry alternatives

Sentry charges based on event volume. Their Team plan covers 50,000 events per month. A single uncaught exception in a loop can burn through that quota in minutes during an incident — exactly when you need error tracking most. Self-hosted alternatives store events on your disk with no volume cap. During a spike, they log every event instead of throttling or invoicing you.

Self-hosted alternatives

Seismograph

Error tracking with deduplication and grouping. Captures exceptions, groups by signature, and tracks resolution. Replaces Sentry's core error tracking without the event volume pricing.

Overview

Chronicle

Event logging with severity filtering and source tracking. Useful as a lightweight error log for applications that do not need full Sentry-style stack trace analysis.

Overview

Quarry

Log aggregation with structured search. If your error tracking needs are met by searchable log entries with severity levels, Quarry handles this without the complexity of a full error tracking platform.

Overview

An honest assessment

Sentry is a genuinely excellent product with source map support, release tracking, performance monitoring, and native SDKs for every major framework. If you need those features and the pricing works for your team, Sentry is a strong choice. Self-hosted alternatives make sense when you need unlimited events, cannot send error payloads to a third party, or your team's SaaS budget is constrained.
