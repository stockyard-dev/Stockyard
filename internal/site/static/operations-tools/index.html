Self-Hosted Operations Tools

Every tool is a single binary with embedded SQLite. No Docker, no Postgres, no external dependencies.

Operations teams handle the most sensitive organizational data: incident timelines, compliance records, internal communications, policy documents, and access logs. Storing this data in third-party SaaS tools creates audit surface that compliance teams then have to manage. Every vendor becomes a data processor under GDPR, a subprocessor under SOC 2, and a risk factor in every security review. Self-hosted operations tools keep this data on infrastructure you control, which simplifies compliance posture and eliminates the vendor dependency chain that makes auditors nervous.

Where to start

For a growing team, start with Handbook (internal wiki), Inquest (incident management), Brand (audit trail), and Announcements (team broadcasts). These cover the operational foundations that become painful to migrate once you have years of history in a SaaS tool.
