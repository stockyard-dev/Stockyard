What Paddock does

HTTP & TCP monitors

Monitor any HTTP endpoint or TCP port. Configurable check intervals from 30 seconds to 1 hour. Tracks response time and status codes.

Instant alerts

Get notified via email, webhook, or Slack the moment a monitor goes down. Configurable thresholds and escalation rules.

Public status pages

Publish a public status page showing your service uptime history. Customizable with your brand. Hosted on your own domain.

Uptime history

90-day uptime history with response time graphs. SLA reporting. Incident timeline with cause and resolution notes.

SSL certificate monitoring

Automatically check SSL certificate expiry and alert you before they expire. Never get caught with an expired cert again.

Single Go binary

One 8MB binary, SQLite storage, no Redis or Postgres required. Runs on any server with a single command. Upgrades are a binary swap.
