The Local tier is the full product.

Local includes every tool, every bundle, every feature that runs on your machine. Cloud Single and Cloud Multi add cloud — they don’t add features. If you don’t want your data leaving your machine, Local is the entire product.

If your Cloud subscription ever lapses, your local features keep working. The data on your machine is yours, period. We never take it away.

Frequently asked questions

Can I try it before I buy?

The proxy platform is free and open-source at stockyard.dev, and every bundle has a detailed tools list you can review before purchase. If you have questions before buying, email hello@stockyard.dev and we’ll answer within a day.

What happens if Stockyard the company disappears?

The Local license has no expiry. The binary keeps working. Your data stays on your machine in standard SQLite files. You can open them with any tool.

What if my Cloud subscription lapses?

Cloud sync stops. Every local feature keeps working. Your data on your machine is never affected.

What’s a “site”?

A site is a logical location. “Downtown Studio” and “Uptown Studio” are two sites with separate data. One business at one address running ten bundles is one site. Most customers are single-site.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes — email hello@stockyard.dev with your license key and we’ll apply a credit for what you’ve already paid toward the new tier.

How does this compare to HoneyBook or Mindbody?

HoneyBook is $348–1,548/year. Mindbody is $1,188+/year per location. Stockyard Local is $299 once — that’s it, no renewal. Cloud Single is $490/year for one location. Cloud Multi is $1,290/year for unlimited locations. We’re cheaper because the software runs on your machine, not our servers.

Stockyard Desktop runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux.
