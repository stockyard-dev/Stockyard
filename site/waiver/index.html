Stockyard Waiver

Self-hosted digital consent forms

Digital waiver and consent form signing with signature capture. Track who signed what and when.

curl -fsSL https://stockyard.dev/waiver/install.sh | sh
How it works

You build your waiver text once, with the legal language your insurer or your state requires. Clients open the form on a phone or tablet (or your iPad at the front desk), read it, sign with their finger, type their name, and submit. The signed waiver gets stored as a PDF with a timestamp, the IP it was submitted from, and the signature image embedded. You can search by name, by date, or by waiver template. You can hand any signed copy to your lawyer or your insurer in seconds because it's all on your machine.

What it doesn't do (read this part)

Waiver is not a substitute for actual legal review. The text in your waiver is whatever you write — Stockyard does not provide templates and does not give legal advice. Your insurer or your state may require specific language, and that part is on you. Waiver also doesn't do witness e-signatures, multi-party contracts, or notarization. If you need DocuSign-style multi-party signing flows, use DocuSign. Waiver is for the simple case: one client, one form, one signature, one local PDF you can produce on demand.

Who it's for

Tattoo studios. Gyms and CrossFit boxes. Yoga and pilates studios. Dance studios. Climbing gyms. Trampoline parks. Anywhere clients walk through the door and you need a signed piece of paper before they do the thing. Veterinary practices for surgical consents. Therapists for HIPAA acknowledgements. Doulas and midwives for birth-plan confirmations. Anyone whose insurer would ask "where's the signed waiver" if something went wrong.

Replaces

Replaces Jotform Sign ($24/mo). Self-hosted. Your data stays on your hardware.
