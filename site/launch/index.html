What you get
  • One AI prompt builds a toolkit personalized to your business — fields, categories, defaults, the works
  • Every tool ships as a single Go binary with embedded SQLite. No Postgres, no Redis, no Docker required
  • Your data lives in ~/stockyard/data/. You can tar -czf it and move it to another machine
  • OpenAI-compatible LLM proxy with provider routing, failover, spend tracking, and 76 middleware modules
  • Open core: the proxy is Apache 2.0. The dashboard, apps, and 29 platform products are BSL 1.1
  • Every AI feature on stockyard.dev runs through our own proxy. Yes, we dogfood it
