The Local tier is a real product, not a trial.

Local includes every tool, every bundle, every feature that runs on your machine. Cloud Single and Cloud Multi add cloud — they don't add features. If you don't want your data leaving your machine, Local is the entire product.

If your cloud subscription ever lapses, your local features keep working. The data on your machine is yours, period. We never take it away.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Without a license the app runs in trial mode with limited functionality. The Local tier is a one-time purchase at $49.99 with all tools and bundles unlocked.

What happens if Stockyard the company disappears?

The Local tier license has no expiry. The binary keeps working. Your data stays on your machine in standard SQLite files. You can open them with any tool.

What if my Cloud subscription lapses?

Cloud sync stops. Every local feature keeps working. Your data on your machine is never affected.

What's a "site"?

A site is a logical location. "Downtown Studio" and "Uptown Studio" are two sites with separate data. One business at one address running ten bundles is one site. Most customers are single-site.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Local to Cloud Single is a prorated upgrade. Cloud Single to Cloud Multi too. Downgrades work in the other direction with no data loss.

How is this different from the per-bundle subscription?

The desktop app replaces it. The old per-bundle subscription model has been retired in favor of one app, all tools, three pricing tiers based on cloud needs.
