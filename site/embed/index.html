Embeddable Widgets

Add Stockyard-powered widgets to your own site with one line of code.

Uptime Badge
Show your service uptime powered by Bellwether. Automatically updates.
<img src="http://YOUR_SERVER:8650/api/badge?service=api" alt="Uptime">
Status Page
Embed a live status indicator powered by Paddock on your homepage or docs.
<iframe src="http://YOUR_SERVER:8680/embed/status" style="width:100%;height:200px;border:none"></iframe>
Feedback Form
Embed a feedback widget powered by Podium. Submissions go straight to your dashboard.
<iframe src="http://YOUR_SERVER:8830/embed/form" style="width:100%;height:300px;border:none"></iframe>
NPS Survey
Embed a Net Promoter Score survey powered by Surveyor II.
<iframe src="http://YOUR_SERVER:8850/embed/nps" style="width:100%;height:180px;border:none"></iframe>
Analytics Pixel
Privacy-first page view tracking powered by Headcount. No cookies, no consent banner.
<script defer src="http://YOUR_SERVER:8690/track.js" data-site="my-site"></script>
Link Shortener Redirect
Use Lasso short links anywhere. No embed needed, just use the short URL.
curl -X POST http://YOUR_SERVER:8780/api/links -d '{"url":"https://example.com","slug":"my-link"}'
# Result: http://YOUR_SERVER:8780/my-link

Replace YOUR_SERVER with your server's IP or domain. All embeds work over HTTP or HTTPS.
