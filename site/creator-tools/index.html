Self-Hosted Creator & Marketing Tools

Every tool is a single binary with embedded SQLite. No Docker, no Postgres, no external dependencies.

Creator and marketing tools sit at the intersection of audience data and content distribution. Your email list, click analytics, content calendar, and engagement metrics describe your audience more precisely than any other data source. When this data lives in Mailchimp, Bitly, or Buffer, you are building your audience profile on rented infrastructure. Platform changes, algorithm shifts, or simple pricing increases can disrupt workflows you depend on. Self-hosted marketing tools let you own your audience data permanently. The subscriber list, the click history, the content archive — all of it stays on your server regardless of what any SaaS vendor decides to do.

Where to start

If you are a content creator or marketer, start with Post (blog engine), Dispatch (email lists), Lasso (link shortening), and Headcount (analytics). These four tools replace the core marketing stack — Ghost, Mailchimp, Bitly, and Google Analytics — for under $4 per month combined.
