The problem

Why most tools need a consent banner.

When you use Bitly, Short.io, or any managed link shortener, every click goes through their servers. They see the referrer, the user agent, the IP address, and the timestamp for every person who clicks your links. This is your audience data — on someone else's infrastructure.

For marketing teams subject to GDPR or data privacy requirements, this third-party processing creates compliance obligations. For anyone who values data ownership, it means giving away valuable audience insights.

How Lasso is different

No cookies. No third-party processing. No banner needed.

  • Clicks are processed on your server — no third-party intermediary
  • Click data (referrer, geo, device) is stored in your own database
  • No third-party cookies or tracking pixels on the redirect
  • Your audience data stays private and under your control
What you track

What you actually get.

Click count, timestamp, referrer URL, user agent (browser/device), country (from IP), and UTM parameters. Pro adds full analytics dashboards, QR code generation, and CSV export.

Legal note
Self-hosted link tracking processes click data on your own infrastructure. Under GDPR, this avoids the third-party processing obligations that come with using external link shorteners. You still need to disclose click tracking in your privacy policy.
FAQ
Does Lasso use cookies?
No. Lasso performs a simple HTTP redirect with no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no JavaScript on the redirect page.
Is this GDPR compliant?
Self-hosted link tracking avoids third-party data transfer. You process click data on your own server. Disclose it in your privacy policy. Consult your legal team for your specific requirements.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes. Pro tier supports custom domains. Point your DNS and Lasso handles the rest. Your short links use your domain, not a third-party brand.
