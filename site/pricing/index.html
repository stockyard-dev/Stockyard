How it works
1

Download the binary

One command: brew install stockyard-dev/tap/stockyard or npx, docker, or curl.

2

Run it

Set your LLM API key, run the binary. Dashboard appears at localhost/ui.

3

Upgrade when you're ready

The free tier is production-ready. Drover autopilot routing is included at 100 requests/day, and Feral quickscan gives you a free 5-probe security audit. When you need unlimited Drover, the full 29-probe Feral suite, Lasso replay, or other products — upgrade to unlock them. Set STOCKYARD_LICENSE_KEY=SY-... and restart.

Common questions
What do I get for free?
The full proxy with all 76 middleware modules, all 40 LLM providers, and unlimited requests. Plus 16 core apps including tracing, audit, prompt studio, workflows, billing, and teams. Self-hosted on your infrastructure. Drover autopilot routing is included at 100 requests/day. Feral quickscan (5-probe security audit) is free. The other 28 products (Lasso, Breed, etc.) unlock with paid tiers.
What's the difference between tiers?
Each tier unlocks more products. The free tier includes Drover autopilot routing at 100 requests/day. Individual ($29.99/mo) adds Lasso replay, Doubt, Verdikt, and 2 more. Pro ($99.99/mo) upgrades Drover to unlimited and adds Breed evolution, Stampede load testing, and 4 more. Team ($299.99/mo) adds Feral red-team, Phantom personas, and 9 more. Enterprise ($499.99/mo) unlocks everything including Cortex, Mycelium, Ramrod, and SSO/SLA.
Can I save with annual billing?
Yes. Annual plans save roughly 2 months: Individual $299.90/yr, Pro $999.90/yr, Team $2,999.90/yr, Enterprise $4,999.90/yr.
How does Team pricing work for more than 5 people?
Team includes 5 seats. Additional seats are $20/seat/month. So a 10-person team is $299.99 + (5 × $20) = $399.99/month.
What providers are supported?
OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, Ollama, DeepSeek, xAI, Mistral, Cohere, Together, Fireworks, Perplexity, Replicate, Amazon Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, and Google Vertex AI. Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works out of the box.
Do I need to change my application code?
No. Just point your OPENAI_BASE_URL at Stockyard. Works with the official OpenAI, Anthropic, and other provider SDKs — zero code changes required.
What does Enterprise include beyond Team?
All 29 products including Cortex, Mycelium, Spore, Molt, Ramrod, and Iron. Plus unlimited seats, SSO/SAML, SLA with uptime guarantees, compliance export, and a dedicated support channel. Email enterprise@stockyard.dev to discuss.
