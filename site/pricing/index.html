How it works
1

Download the binary

One command: brew install stockyard-dev/tap/stockyard or npx, docker, or curl.

2

Run it

Set your LLM API key, run the binary. Dashboard appears at localhost/ui.

3

Upgrade when you're ready

The free tier works forever. When you need more requests, modules, or providers — upgrade and get a license key. Set STOCKYARD_LICENSE_KEY=SY-... and restart.

Common questions
What do I get for free?
Everything. All 58 modules, all 16 apps, all 16 providers, unlimited requests. Self-hosted on your infrastructure. Free forever. You can also publish apps, contribute to the mesh, and sell knowledge bases on the free tier — and earn from all of them.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free is self-hosted — you run the binary yourself. Pro is cloud-managed — we host it for you with zero ops, custom domain, auto backups, 90-day audit retention, and priority support. Both have the same features and marketplace access.
Can I save with annual billing?
Yes. Annual plans give you 2 months free: Pro $290/yr (save $58), Team $990/yr (save $198), Enterprise $2,990/yr (save $598).
How does Team pricing work for more than 5 people?
Team includes 5 seats. Additional seats are $20/seat/month. So a 10-person team is $99 + (5 × $20) = $199/month.
How do I earn money on the platform?
Three ways. Publish AI apps to the marketplace and earn 80% of every transaction. Contribute GPU compute to the mesh and earn 88% of inference fees. Sell knowledge bases and earn 80% of query royalties. All three work on every tier including Free.
What providers are supported?
OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, Ollama, DeepSeek, xAI, Mistral, Cohere, Together, Fireworks, Perplexity, Replicate, Amazon Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, and Google Vertex AI. Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works out of the box. Plus the distributed mesh for open-source model inference.
Do I need to change my application code?
No. Just point your OPENAI_BASE_URL at Stockyard. Works with the official OpenAI, Anthropic, and other provider SDKs — zero code changes required.
What does Enterprise include beyond Team?
Unlimited seats, Trust federation, SSO/SAML, SLA with uptime guarantees, safety certification, compliance export, unlimited audit retention, and a dedicated support channel. Email enterprise@stockyard.dev to discuss.
